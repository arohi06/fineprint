How it works

Three quiet steps.

01

Fetch

We visit the site, find its privacy policy, terms and cookie notice, and list every third-party script loading in the background.

02

Read

A language model sorts every sentence: data selling, tracking, retention, rights waivers, and the good stuff too, like opt-outs and encryption.

03

Score

You get a 0-100 privacy score, a plain-English summary, the exact clauses that matter and a checklist of what to do about them.
